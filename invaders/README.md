# Invaders

8-bit Space Invaders tribute for iPhone. Plain HTML5 canvas + WebAudio, no build step, no dependencies.

- Play: open `invaders/` in Safari (GitHub Pages: `/invaders/`), then Share > Add to Home Screen for full-screen.
- Touch: left/right pads (slide your thumb between them), hold FIRE to shoot, II pauses, the note toggles sound.
- Keyboard: arrows or A/D, Space to fire, P pause, M mute.
- Arcade details kept: 224x256 screen, 55-alien march that speeds up as they thin, 4-note bass beat, erodable bunkers,
  mystery ship (the 23rd shot and every 15th after scores 300), extra life at 1500, colored cellophane strips.
